How it works

You enter a question. Phiversity plans the explanation, renders the animations, adds narration, and assembles a lecture video you can watch and revisit from your library.

Questions

Which subjects are supported?

Mathematics, physics, chemistry, and economics.

Do I need an API key?

Bundled sample lectures work without one. Generating a lecture from your own question needs an LLM key configured on the server.

What if a generation fails?

The job reports what went wrong and you can submit it again.
